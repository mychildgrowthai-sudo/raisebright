Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

RaiseBright is intended for parents and legal guardians. Children should not submit personal information directly.

Information we use

We may process purchaser contact and order information, together with parent-provided details needed to personalize a plan for a child ages 3–12. These details may include the child's first name, age, interests, personality descriptors, strengths, parent-selected goals, and available daily time.

Please do not submit information that is not needed to create the plan.

How we use information

Information is used to process and support orders, personalize and deliver the requested plan, operate and secure the service, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Legal bases where applicable

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, personal information may be processed because it is necessary to provide the product you requested, because you have given consent for an optional activity such as analytics, because we must comply with a legal obligation, or because we have a legitimate interest in operating, securing, and supporting the service where that interest does not override your rights.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use RaiseBright. Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose to accept analytics in the consent banner.

If you reject analytics or do not accept them, our GA4 implementation does not load Google Analytics and does not transmit analytics data to Google.

Analytics information may include pages viewed and general device/browser and usage information. Questionnaire answers and information about your child are not sent to Google Analytics.

Your analytics choice is stored in your browser for this site. To reset that choice, you can clear RaiseBright's stored site data in your browser; the consent banner will then be shown again on a future visit.

Data retention

Order and purchaser contact information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill and support the order and to meet applicable accounting, fraud-prevention, record-keeping, or other legal obligations.

Questionnaire information relating to a child is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to prepare and deliver the personalized plan, provide related support, and meet applicable legal requirements. Retention is reviewed according to the purpose for which the information is held.

Children's privacy

A parent or legal guardian must submit the questionnaire. Children should not submit information directly.

Please do not submit medical records, diagnoses, photographs, home or school addresses, school records, identification documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information about a child.

Service providers

Payments are processed securely through PayPal, our authorized payment provider. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

We also use third-party services as necessary to operate the questionnaire, automation, personalization, document generation, email delivery, analytics, security, and support functions of RaiseBright. These providers may process only the information needed for the functions they perform for the service.

Current service providers may include Tally for questionnaire collection, Make for workflow automation, and Google services for personalization, document generation, analytics, and email-related functions.

International processing

Some service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers of personal information, we rely on the safeguards made available by the relevant service providers and applicable law.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict or object to certain processing, request data portability, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where another lawful reason permits its continued retention.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a privacy request.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email support@raisebright.org. If your request concerns an existing order, including the email address used for the order can help us locate it. Please do not send unnecessary information about your child.
