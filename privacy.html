Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

RaiseBright is intended for parents and legal guardians. Children should not submit personal information directly.

Information used

We may process purchaser contact and order information plus parent-provided details needed to personalize a plan for a child ages 3–12, including age, interests, personality descriptors, strengths, goals, and available daily time.

Purpose

Information is used for order processing, personalization, delivery, support, security, and legal obligations.

Children’s privacy

A parent or guardian must submit the questionnaire. Do not submit medical records, diagnoses, photographs, addresses, school records, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Providers

Payments are processed through Lemon Squeezy. Google Analytics processes limited website usage information only when analytics consent has been provided. Other providers may process information only as needed for questionnaire and fulfillment functions.

Contact

Use the support contact shown on the order receipt. Do not send unnecessary information about a child.
