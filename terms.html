Product and purchaser eligibility

RaiseBright provides a personalized 30-day digital growth plan, delivered as a PDF, for parents and legal guardians of children ages 3–12. The plan is prepared from information submitted through the parent questionnaire and includes 30 daily activities, parent guidance, suggested materials, weekly reflection prompts, and gentler alternatives.

You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of legal majority where you live, to make a purchase. No specific developmental, educational, behavioral, or health outcome is guaranteed.

Adult responsibility and safety

A responsible adult must review every activity, decide whether it is appropriate for the child, provide age-appropriate supervision, and adapt or stop any activity that causes discomfort, distress, or appears unsuitable or unsafe.

Before an activity begins, the adult should check the materials and environment for age-appropriate risks, including choking, allergies, sharp objects, slipping, or other foreseeable hazards.
