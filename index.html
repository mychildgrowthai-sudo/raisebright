Common questions

FAQ

Is this therapy? +

No. RaiseBright is an educational support tool that offers development-focused activities. It is not therapy and is not a medical or diagnostic service.

How old should my child be? +

RaiseBright is designed for parents of children ages 3 to 12, with guidance and activities personalized around the child's age, stage, interests, strengths, and parent-selected goals.

How much time does it take? +

Activity length varies based on your child's age and the time you can realistically dedicate. Each activity includes practical timing guidance, with flexibility to shorten, repeat, or extend it when appropriate.

Do I need special materials? +

No. Activities are designed to use things most families already have at home.
