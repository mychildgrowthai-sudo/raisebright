Refund Policy

We want every RaiseBright purchase to feel clear and straightforward. This policy explains how refund requests, delivery problems, duplicate purchases, and other purchase concerns are handled.

Last updated: August 7, 2026.
Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory consumer rights that apply to you under the laws of your country or region.

1. What you are purchasing

RaiseBright provides personalized digital educational-support materials based on information submitted through the parent or caregiver questionnaire.

The current RaiseBright product is a personalized 30-day digital growth plan delivered electronically. It is an educational support resource and is not a medical, psychological, diagnostic, or therapeutic service.

2. Our approach to refund requests

Because each RaiseBright plan is prepared using the information provided for a specific child profile, work may begin shortly after the completed questionnaire is received.

If something goes wrong with your purchase, please contact us. We review refund requests fairly and individually rather than relying on an automatic “no refunds” rule.

Where applicable law gives you additional cancellation, withdrawal, refund, repair, replacement, or other consumer rights, those rights remain unaffected by this policy.

3. When a refund may be appropriate

A full or partial refund may be considered when, for example:

  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
  • a technical or payment error resulted in an unintended purchase;
  • we are unable to provide the purchased plan;
  • the plan was not delivered and the issue cannot be resolved after you contact us;
  • the product delivered is materially different from what was described at the time of purchase;
  • another refund right applies under applicable consumer law.

4. Personalized digital content

RaiseBright plans are personalized digital products. Once meaningful preparation of a personalized plan has begun or the completed plan has been delivered, a refund may not always be appropriate simply because you changed your mind.

However, we do not use this provision to remove rights that applicable consumer law gives you. Eligibility will always be considered in light of the circumstances of the purchase and any mandatory legal rights.

5. Delivery problems

Personalized plans are normally prepared after the questionnaire has been completed. If you believe your plan is overdue, first check the email address used during purchase and your spam or junk folder.

If the plan has still not arrived, contact us at support@raisebright.org. We will investigate the delivery issue and work to resolve it before treating the order as undelivered.

6. Duplicate or accidental charges

If you believe you were charged twice for the same purchase, or a payment was processed incorrectly, contact us as soon as possible and include the email address used at checkout and the relevant order information.

Please do not send full card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials by email.

7. How to request a refund or purchase review

Email support@raisebright.org with:

  • the email address used for the purchase;
  • your order number or receipt information, if available;
  • a short explanation of the issue;
  • any information that will help us understand and resolve the problem.

Please protect your family's privacy. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information about your child, medical records, passwords, full payment-card details, or other confidential information in a refund request.

8. Payment processing and Lemon Squeezy

RaiseBright purchases are processed through Lemon Squeezy. Lemon Squeezy acts as the merchant of record for transactions processed through its platform and handles payment processing and related transaction functions.

Depending on the circumstances, a refund may be processed through Lemon Squeezy. The time required for an approved refund to appear on your payment method can depend on the payment provider and banking system.

9. Chargebacks

If you have a problem with an order, we encourage you to contact us first so we have an opportunity to investigate and resolve it.

Nothing in this section prevents you from exercising any lawful rights available through your payment provider or applicable consumer law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy as RaiseBright develops, our products change, or legal and payment-processing requirements evolve.

The version published on this page will show the date of the most recent update. Changes will apply prospectively unless applicable law requires otherwise.

11. Contact us

Questions about a purchase or this policy can be sent to:
