Contact RaiseBright

We are here to help with questions about RaiseBright, an order or the delivery of your personalized guidance plan.

Email: support@raisebright.org

What we can help with

  • Questions about RaiseBright before purchasing
  • Order or payment-related questions
  • A guidance plan that has not arrived
  • Duplicate purchases, corrections or refund reviews
  • Technical problems using the website
  • Privacy, access or deletion requests

What to include

Please briefly describe the issue. For order support, include the email address used for the purchase and the PayPal transaction ID, if available. Do not send your PayPal password, payment-card details or security codes.

Protect your family’s privacy

Please do not email medical records, diagnoses, photographs, home or school addresses, identification documents or other unnecessary sensitive information about a child or family member.

RaiseBright is intended for parents and legal guardians. Children should not contact RaiseBright or provide information independently.

Response time

We aim to respond within 2–3 business days. Complex payment, privacy or delivery investigations may require additional time.

Urgent or professional help

This email is not monitored for emergencies. RaiseBright provides educational guidance and does not provide medical, psychological or emergency services. If urgent help is needed, contact the appropriate local emergency service or a qualified professional.

For information about how personal information is handled, please read our Privacy Policy.
