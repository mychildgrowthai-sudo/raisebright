About RaiseBright

Every child grows in their own way. Parents often know what they would like to support, but generic advice does not always fit their child or everyday family life.

RaiseBright is an independent educational project designed to provide personalized 30-day parent guidance plans for parents and legal guardians of children ages 3–12.

Each plan is shaped around information the parent chooses to provide, such as the child’s age, interests, personality, strengths, goals and daily routine. This information is turned into clear, age-appropriate activities that parents can guide at home.

Our approach

  • Personalized around the individual child
  • Guided and used by the parent or legal guardian
  • Practical for everyday family routines
  • Built around age-appropriate activities
  • Educational rather than clinical

How plans are prepared

RaiseBright uses automated tools, including AI, to help personalize and prepare each guidance plan based on information provided by a parent or legal guardian. The resulting plan is educational and does not provide medical, psychological, diagnostic or therapeutic advice.

Who RaiseBright is for

RaiseBright is intended for parents and legal guardians. It is not designed for children to use or complete independently.

Parents should not submit medical records, diagnoses, photographs, home or school addresses, identification documents or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Important scope

RaiseBright provides educational guidance and practical activities for families.

It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, psychological treatment or clinical services, and it is not a replacement for support from a qualified healthcare, educational or mental-health professional when such support is needed.
